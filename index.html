Manual Step-by-Step Flow

If you prefer to read through the logic manually, follow the step-by-step table below.

Step 1: Do you need to take it on the road (RV) or need a weather backup?
  • If Yes → Pitch: Starlink (Perfect for travel or backup)
  • If No → Proceed to Step 2
Step 2: Do you currently live in a house or an apartment?
  • If Apartment → Pitch: Boost PPI / Cable (Starlink is rarely an option)
  • If House → Proceed to Step 3
Step 3: Can I grab your address real quick to check what's available?
  • If Rural → Pitch: Starlink (Best for outside cable zones)
  • If City/Suburb → Proceed to Step 4
Step 4: How many devices do you have, and is anyone a heavy gamer?
  • If Basic / Budget → Pitch: Boost PPI (Lowest price, strictly prepaid). Tip: Ask if they are a Boost Mobile customer for extra discounts!
  • If Heavy Use Gamer → Proceed to Step 5
Step 5: What is the layout of your home? Is it large or L-shaped?
  • If Yes → Add-On: Pitch Spectrum + Advanced WiFi & Pods (eliminate dead zones)
  • If No → Base Spectrum Gig plan is perfect

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why is Starlink rarely an option for apartment complexes?
A: Starlink requires a clear, unobstructed view of the sky to connect to satellites. Most apartment renters do not have roof access, and balcony views are often blocked by overhangs or neighboring buildings.

Q: What if the customer is unsure if they are a "heavy gamer" or "basic user"?
A: Ask them what they use the internet for most. If they just check email, use social media, and watch Netflix on one TV, they are a Basic User. If they have multiple kids playing Xbox/PlayStation online while parents stream 4K video, they are a Heavy User.

Q: Can I skip right to Step 3 and just check their address?
A: It's always best to ask Step 1 and Step 2 first. Building rapport and understanding their living situation (like if they travel in an RV) helps you pitch the value of the service, rather than just reading a list of what's available.
